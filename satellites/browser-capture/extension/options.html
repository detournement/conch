Conch Browser Capture

Capture happens only on origins listed below — nothing is recorded anywhere else, and nothing is recorded at all until you add one. Clicks are captured as semantic targets (role/label), form submits as field names only, copy events without content; password and secret-looking fields are never captured. Events go to your local Conch kernel over native messaging. No cloud.
